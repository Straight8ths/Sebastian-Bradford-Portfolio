# Pure Math Sandbox

A collection of mathematical thought-starters and mental chew toys, made with Python and Pandas.

## Contents

**Division Decimals**
- Cataloguing common (and rare) decimal values via division.

**Multiplication Terminal Digits**
- Exploring the surprising behavior of ordinary multiplication.

**Prime Rays**
- A visual exploration of prime factors.

**Primes in Powers**
- Analyzing primes with respect to bases and exponents.

**Relative Prime Positions**
- Examining the relative positions of primes with respect to their neighbors.
